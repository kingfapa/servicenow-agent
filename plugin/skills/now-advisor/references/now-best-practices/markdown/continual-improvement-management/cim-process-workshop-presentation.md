### Speaker notes

Getting started with continual service improvement (CSI) using the ServiceNow Continual Improvement Management (CIM) application is simple and can significantly enhance the value you provide to your customers. However, following some best practice guidance is vital for successful implementation.

Lasting change doesn’t depend on large-scale events.

First and foremost, lasting change doesn’t depend on large-scale events or paradigm shifts. Instead, effective continual improvement often involves implementing small, incremental, more practical, and inexpensive steps.

Make improvement a regular habit and not a one-time goal.

Employees are indispensable to the continual improvement process.

Another important principle of the continuous improvement (CI) process is the recognition that employees play a vital role. By involving them in the process, we can access their unique knowledge and expertise, and empower them to implement their ideas. Open communication and feedback are crucial components for the success of this approach.

Any improvements should be measurable.

Any enhancements should be quantifiable, allowing a company to evaluate, replicate, and implement the procedure in other business areas.

Distribute responsibility for CSI throughout the organization.

Improvement of services and processes falls on the shoulders of everyone involved, not just the CSI Manager. To make things simpler, the CIM application offers various roles. It's wise for the improvement manager to delegate improvement coordinator roles to other process owners or improvement teams.

Align your improvement with strategy

Some companies continue to make the mistake of making improvements in silos without doing a proper alignment with business objectives and goals. The CIM application solves this problem by breaking down silos and aligning initiatives with business objectives using a central table on the Now Platform. By aligning improvement initiatives with strategic objectives before moving them to the implementation stage, the CIM application helps businesses achieve their goals effectively.

### Speaker notes

The operational baseline is the currently defined acceptable level of service, for example:

 I am able to access internet banking between 09:00 - 17:00. If something occurs below the standard operational baseline (see graphic below), then Incident Management is the process to manage the return to the baseline, for example internet banking is unavailable.

A change would be raised to perform the action to return to the operational baseline. If an improvement initiative/candidate is identified (for example internet banking should be available 24/7) this would potentially set a new baseline.

The improvement initiative registers the improvement and sets the tasks and measures of success (compare it to a mini-project). Once the initiative has been approved for implementation, a change (or series of changes) would be raised to complete the required actions to make the improvement live. From there a new baseline for the service/application/process etc. is set from which future impact/improvement is measured.

Explain and discuss

Continual Improvement (or Continuous Improvement) is an ongoing improvement that happens in stages. It is a long-term approach to improve services, processes, products, and functions. This tested method occurs gradually and aims to make small incremental changes over time. Continual Improvement looks to change and improve business services or processes to increase efficiency and reduce costs.

Dictionary Excursion: What is the difference between continuous and continual?

Continual means repeated but with breaks in between; chronic. Example: The continual problem of our car's not starting forced us to sell it. Continuous means without interruption in an unbroken stream of time or space.

Continual Improvement and Integration with ITSM/SPM Apps

Does CIM has any dependency on Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM) App (Demand, Projects)?

No. CIM App has no hard dependency on SPM Apps. The out of the box integration points will show up automatically when both CIM App and SPM App plugins are installed.

What is the difference between a Demand/Project and an Improvement Initiative?

Demand Module: The Demand Management application consists of tools for capturing, centralizing, and assessing strategic and operational demands: idea, projects, needs during the creation of portfolio.

Who uses it? Demand users create idea or demand request directly. Demand manager assess and prioritize the demand and convert it to a project, enhancement, change, or defect.

Project Module: A project is temporary in that it has a defined beginning and end in time, and therefore defined scope and resources. The ServiceNow® Project Management application is a suite of tools that aids in managing projects, tasks, and resources. Projects supports functionalities like WBS, automated and hybrid projects, resource management, cost management, budgeting etc.

Who use it: Project Managers, Portfolio Managers

Improvement Initiative: Continual Improvement is not a typical project with a start and end date – it is continual. You collect improvement opportunities in to an Improvement register. Improvement initiative go through a plan, do, check, act (PDCA) like process flow. At the end of CI PDCA cycle, the implemented measures should be reinforced by standards that make the improvement permanent (avoid rollbacks)

Continual Improvement Management module does not provide demand/project specific features like business case management, resource/budget/cost managements.

Who use it: Improvement Manager, Improvement practitioners, IT Staff who interact with IT Service/Processes, Analysts, SPM

### Speaker notes

Explain and discuss

Explain that this is the out of the box lifecycle

Explain that our core process is very simple and that is a deliberate design decision to try to move customers away from unnecessary and complex customizations. Experience shows us that many customers who believe they needed to add more complexity to this process have since tried to remove it

States in any ServiceNow application serve a specific purpose. They are designed to make it clear where a record in a specific process currently resides and to display progress.

States should represent a unique phase in a process where a specific set of related activities are grouped together designed to achieve a particular outcome in order to move to the next phase of the process.

Recommend

Strongly push back on any customer that wants to alter this state model – there must be very compelling reasons for doing so. This is because ServiceNow has designed a core process that is proven to work for all customers regardless of size, industry or maturity. In addition many features of the process would need to be reworked if the state model was to change. All new enhancements and features coming in future releases will be designed around the out of the box state model so making any change to this will cause a lot of potential reconfiguring to happen in new releases or possibly for features to simply not work or not be available to the customer as a result

## Slide 70

Analytics

 Three ways to measure

Using KPIs-related financial results (Revenue increase, cost-saving, etc.)

Using survey and assessment (Assess before and after)

As the success is viewed by the stakeholders (Alignment with business objectives)

To maximize the benefits of the CIM initiative, Performance Analytics (PA) is a crucial tool. The Improvement coordinator can utilize PA KPIs to monitor the progress of continuous improvement on a trend chart. By using PA KPIs for improvement tracking, it becomes easier to set SMART goals and showcase the outcome of business value.

Indirect Return on Investment (ROI) may be harder to financially quantify but is equally important to long-term growth and success. Examples of metrics that can be used.

Time saving: Faster process execution, product fulfillment, and shorter outage time = increase in satisfaction and revenue

Product/Service quality: Better products = happier employees and customers AND higher retention.

Compliance: Protect your brand and reputation in the eyes of stakeholders and customers.

Safety - Employee, customer, or vendor: Fewer accidents = lower costs.

Satisfaction - Employee or customer.

Engagement - Employee or customer.

### Speaker notes

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) evaluate the success of a particular activity toward meeting the critical success factors. Successfully managing KPIs can be either through repeatedly meeting an objective (maintain) or by making progress toward an objective (increase/decrease). The Benchmarks feature gives you instant visibility into your key performance indicators (KPIs) and trends, as well as comparative insight relative to industry averages of your peers. You can contrast the performance of your organization with recognized industry standards and view a side-by-side comparison of performance with global benchmarks.

The main point of note when creating any KPIs or metrics for problem management is not to be driven by the same measurements used for incident management. With incidents, the purpose of the process is to restore service as quickly as possible using whatever means available. Therefore, speed of resolution is a key measurement for this process. With problem management, the purpose is to understand the underlying cause of issues and permanently fix them no matter how long that takes or if it is even possible. Therefore, in problem management, speed of resolution is not something that should be measured. This would drive the wrong behavior for the process and focus on closing records rather than finding the permanent fix. Process Owners need to feel comfortable with problem records potentially remaining open for months or even years.

The CIM process has a number of defined stages to progress the improvement initiative through

The Improvement Register is key component of the CIM process providing the capability to capture all the possible opportunities to make things better and then prioritize the ones to action first (and potentially at all).

Who can raise an improvement?

Ideally anyone in the organization , some examples are

How can improvement requests be raised?

There are a number of ways due to the integration of CIM into other ServiceNow processes

Directly within the CIM application in ServiceNow

Via the Idea Portal\*

From an Incident, Problem, Services under SPM, Survey result, Idea, Demand, Project, CMDB Health, GRC audit review, Performance Analytics, Process Optimization or Benchmark

Automatically via integrations

What ways can improvement opportunities be identified to add into the register? Here are a few suggestions

Carry out regular assessments of your services, processes and technology. Review service reports for trends and issues that need to be addressed.

Look at process KPIs and any internal process assessments in Performance Analytics and platform reports.

Compare with Benchmarks baseline and use the system suggested proven best practice based recommendations.

Ask Process Managers and Owners for improvement insights – they usually have a list.

Review problem &amp; change management reports for trends and issues that need to be addressed

Review Risk registers &amp; GRC Audit test failures to see if there are any improvement opportunities there

Ask the wider IT staff, development and project management teams for improvement suggestions.

Finally provide CI requestor roles so users can submit the improvement request into CI register with one click.

Once the improvement initiatives are logged start by looking for improvement opportunities with High benefit, High urgency and Low cost.

CIM App uses Performance Analytics's Spotlight feature to automatically rank the improvement backlog that enables you to pick the most worthwhile item from the top of the list.

Opportunities with Low benefit and High cost can usually be deferred without further consideration. The remaining opportunities can be ranked by benefit, cost and urgency to enable you to select the most worthwhile

The  'Workbench Dashboard' in CIM App allows you to manage and monitor improvement register as a sprint and backlog. This combination of Agile and IT Service Management is usually very effective at helping you to realize continual and incremental improvements in the services you deliver to your users.

In summary it’s better to start a few improvements and invest the resources needed to complete them fairly quickly than to take on too much and see things stall and fail to make progress.

### Speaker notes

Benefits achieved from continual improvement can be put into two categories:

1. Direct ROI items that can be measured using a KPI and can be converted to a dollar value easily. Examples include cost saving, time saving, revenue generation, and productivity increase.

2. Indirect ROI items that are harder to quantify in dollars, but equally important to long-term growth and success. Examples include improved safety, reduced risk, and improved compliance.

So, to calculate the real value of improvement you should focus on capturing both direct ROI + indirect ROI.

Generally, the goals revolve around the following three KPI buckets:

Impact

Engagement

Activity level

Impact: Around cost, time and revenue Growth, etc. You can attach one or more KPIs to an improvement initiative.

Engagement: User engagement summary, participation by business service or process. With more engagement , you can collect more ideas and make more improvements at all levels in the organization.

Activity Level: Around number, types, frequency and execution of improvements

The CIM Dashboard and using a Performance Analytics based KPI as an automated measurement method provides an easy way to see the metrics related to the various buckets. Once the KPI is attached, you can see the current state and value. Set your goal for the future state and by when you think you can meet your goals in the target review date.

Having the KPIs provided in a visible and transparent way helps provide an increased sense of accountability that drives higher user engagement.

Its critical that  improvement initiative align with the business strategy so that  the executive team can see what improvements have been done in the last three or six months to meet the strategic goals.

CIM provides the ability to associate improvement initiatives with business strategies and see them reported on the CIM Dashboard.

Calculating the direct ROI of improvement is a key metric in both establishing credibility and assessing the savings.

Improved processes make your employees’ time more valuable to your business.

For Example, in the Incident Management Process:

\*  Assume agent’s salary amounts to $20 per hour.

\*  Current state: Agent has the capacity to process 2 incidents per hour, costing the organization $10 per incident.

\*  After Process Improvement: Agents are now able to process 5 incidents per hour, with an average cost of $4.

\*  If your organization processes 100,000 incidents per year, the cost improvement was $50,000.

ROI = 600,000 / 50,000 \*100

\*  ROI = 1200%

Calculating indirect ROI is important and examples of those are

\*Improved quality: The improvement in service quality and convenience will make customers happier, attributing to higher customer retention.

\*Improved safety: customer, staff, patient, and vendor safety (for example, fewer accidents = lower costs).

\*Improved compliance: Maintaining regulatory compliance protects your brand’s reputation in the eyes of stakeholders and customers.

\*Reduced Risk: Improved processes typically offer better security and risk mitigation.

\*Increased levels of employee/customer engagement.

Setting these measurable goals is key in gaining approval to move forwards with the initiative and CIM provides that value driven approach to support the improvement cycle.

CIM structured improvement framework can be used to improve your people and team's performance. Review and improve the skills and competence of your people, to ensure that IT personnel have the right skills and competence to manage the technology, execute the processes, and deliver the services. Create initiatives to provide the required coaching/training to fill skills gaps and make your team profoundly better.

In Summary, measuring, tracking, and reporting on continuous improvement is the single most important thing you can do to ensure that your organization keeps a competitive edge above the competition. Measure both the direct ROI and indirect ROI items to show the total outcome achieved.
