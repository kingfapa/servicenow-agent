### Speaker notes

A lot has changed in the past few years, and companies are pressured to accommodate this new world of work. According to Gartner, CIOs around the world are pressured to determine how they rearchitect their service model to adapt to a hybrid work model, how they drive innovation and continual improvement on distributed teams, and how they increase the Digital dexterity of your workforce,

WFO is a one-stop shop for IT managers, as well as individuals who act in that capacity, such as team leads or supervisors, to manage, monitor, and mentor their IT teams.

WFO provides the required context to managers in one place, thus enabling Managers to focus on their main goals such as how do I improve our performance as a team and how do I improve the performance of individuals.

Workforce optimization is an umbrella product that encompasses multiple modules, including Schedules, Coaching with Learning, Team Performance, Channel Routing, and Skills Management.

Each of these modules can be implemented independently and provides great value to the customer. Nevertheless, all these modules work cohesively to provide maximum value to the customer

### Speaker notes

Workforce Optimization is a solution for both team managers and their team members alike. However, that is just a few of the workflows you can execute on WFO. In the Manager WS, we also have the:

Channels module – this module is built on top of AWA and can be used to manage high-volume L1 service desks managing incidents and interactions. This is great to assign work to tier 1 agents since the decision of assignment is automated using AWA. As a manager, you can view monitor all the incidents and interactions coming in via all your service channels, such as walk-up, virtual agent, phones. Etc. and jump into conversations or escalation to help your team members

The Work scheduler module – which we saw in the demo, is used to manage work items that can be planned and require the human to make the decision of assigning to work item due to it’s sensitivity and/or complexity. This module is great for L2 and L3 service desks that manage multiple types of work items and work on multi-day/week tasks

The Scheduling module allows managers to ensure they have coverage as they need it around the clock –you can create and publish shifts so your team members know their shifts and breaks. In addition, if you need to manage on-call scheduling, it’s integrated with our on-call scheduling app so that managers can create on-call schedules from the manager ws. On top of that, you can manage time off requests, shift swap requests and model different demand scenarios to ensure you have coverage for outlier events.

The Teams module allows you to set your primary team KPIs and monitor your team and your team members against it. You can drill down into the child KPIs that drive the parent KPI and view all the source records that were used to generate that KPI value. You can create and track performance targets, as  you saw, and have a 360 view of the agents work profile – shared across WFO and SOW

Last but not least is the Coaching module where we saw how Ingrid manages skills and learning on her team, but in addition to that, you can have a centralized view of all the skills all your team members have, create coaching assessments s you can provide direct feedback to your team member on the quality of the work items and the team member can provide feedback on the effectiveness of the coaching.

These are the main modules we have in the product and each of these modules has its own set of roles so that if team managers have team leads or supervisor who may do some or one of these activities, our granularity in roles allows that freedom. E.g. if you have a coach that sits outside your team but as a manager, you only want to give them visibility into the coaching module for only the teams they are coach for, you can do that. To take that one step further, a customer does not have to implement all the modules at once to g—live. They can ramp up in a phased approach – we have customers who started phase 1 with scheduling and coaching and then ramped up to the other modules

Maximize productivity by managing and assigning all types of work items in one place in the Work Scheduler module

Close the talent and skills gaps via our skills capabilities and the learning modules

Connect  with their distributed teams by pushing skill review requests and learning tasks to her team members in Service Operations Workspace

Implementation phased approach and easy enough to self-implement. We’ve had a couple of customers self-implement, which we don’t condone but it’s a testament to how easy it is to setup

Granularity in roles so they are allowed to administer different outcomes

This module has the following configurable pieces:

FSM Shift Scheduling configuration switch

Workforce Optimization

A configuration switch is added as part of the FSM Administration Configuration module to enable or disable usage of WFO for shift scheduling

On toggle, a confirmation modal window is shown that allows the admin to decide is they want to switch on/off the feature

Decision Required:

Determine if you want to use WFO for shift scheduling

Recommended to use WFO shift scheduling

With WFO for shift scheduling enabled agents will be picked up from WFO and used for agent determination in Dynamic scheduling.

For Dynamic Scheduling to pick up default schedule when an agent has no schedule defined, it is necessary to maintain a WFO-specific default schedule; otherwise, a default schedule that is already defined will be picked up.

### Speaker notes

Explain and discuss

A configuration switch is added as part of the FSM Administration Configuration module to enable or disable usage of WFO for shift scheduling

On toggle, a confirmation modal window is shown that allows the admin to decide is they want to switch on/off the feature

In the case of the switch disabled, schedules for agents will be picked from FSM data module like before

When gent Shift Scheduling is ON ensure that the assigned default work schedule in dynamic scheduling configuration points to the work schedule created in Manager Workspace

When WFO plugin is active and switch is turned ON multiday task functionality in dynamic scheduling is currently handled. Multi day tasks core functionality has not been modified to work with WFO in v1.0

Switch Behavior and Usage

- FSM WFO switch in the FSM configuration page appears in the UI only when FSM WFO plugin is active

- If the plugin is active and the switch is not turned ON then all the backend modules continue to rely on the work schedules maintained in FSM tables
