## 1. Introduction

Completing this questionnaire will help gather information used to estimate the implementation scope for your ServiceNow IT Service Management (ITSM) Solution deployment.

To complete this questionnaire, strategic, technical, and operational business representatives must be engaged to ensure that new concepts are considered while meeting daily operational needs.

We understand you may have already answered some of these questions with our sales team. However, the implementation team must obtain your responses directly to ensure we have the most up-to-date information and accurately estimate the effort required to configure the ServiceNow solution for your organization’s unique goals and objectives.

Based on your responses, additional meetings may be required to ensure clarity and to answer any questions you may have while completing this questionnaire. If time does not allow, you can skip a question and make a note in the response column; we will address it in a planned meeting.

We look forward to working with you collaboratively during this process.

## 4. Catalog &amp; Request Management Application

Service catalog management aims to provide and maintain a single source of consistent information on all operational services and those being prepared for operational use, and to make it widely available to authorized users.

A service request is a user’s request for information, advice, a standard change (a pre-approved change that is low-risk, relatively common, and follows a procedure), or access to an IT service.

## 5. Incident Management Application

An incident is an unplanned interruption, a reduction in the quality of an IT service, or a failure of a configuration item (CI) that has not yet impacted an IT service. Incident Management aims to restore normal service operations as quickly as possible and minimize the adverse impact of incidents on business operations, thus ensuring that the best possible service quality and availability levels are maintained.

## 6. Problem Management Application

A problem is the cause of one or more incidents.  The root cause is not usually known when a problem record is created, and the Problem Management process is responsible for further investigation.  A known error is a problem where a root cause and an acceptable workaround have been identified, but a permanent resolution has not yet been identified and put in place.

## 9. Employee Center

The Employee Center is a unified employee portal available to all customers. Included in all implementations (unless specifically excluded) are:

- Branding of the Employee Center using standard capabilities for in-scope ServiceNow Solutions (e.g., IT, HR, Workplace, Legal, etc.) with SSO
- Modification of standard Page Layouts, including rearrangement of standard widgets on standard pages using the Service Portal Designer
- Configuration of curated experiences capabilities, which include:
    - One (1) megamenu/taxonomy per solution
    - Configuration of standard dynamic pages included for in-scope solution(s) while training customer System Administrators to create, change, and update dynamic pages is ongoing.
    - One (1) global header
- AI Search with up to two (2) indexed sources, four (4) search sources, two (2) search applications, genius results, stop word dictionary, and synonym dictionary, per solution.
- Configuration of standard Employee Center capabilities (e.g., quick link widget, "More" menu items, footer, active items widget (only hiding unused options), events calendar, and favorites, etc.)
- Configure up to ten (10) Catalog Content Items (e.g., a catalog item that connects the user to either a knowledge article, specific content, or a third-party hyperlink).
- Activate/configure the standard 'Granular Delegation' capabilities with up to three (3) delegation rules.
- Activate/configure the standard 'Employee's Profile' ServiceNow Store application.
- Activation of User Experience Analytics capabilities

## 15. Integrations

A ServiceNow integration is an information exchange between the Now Platform® and another system or source. Integrations are crucial for implementing digital workflows and delivering seamless user experiences. ServiceNow integrations support three use cases:

- Process integrations move data across systems to facilitate a process, like sending ServiceNow records to Jira to support a development process.
- UI integrations present public web information within the Now Platform, such as displaying weather forecasts.
- Data integrations enable data sharing, such as viewing demographic HR data, like titles or managers, within the platform.

The Now Platform supports the most common integration technologies, including but not limited to web services, file retrieval/import sets, JDBC connections, LDAP, REST and SOAP, Excel, CSV, and e-mail transmissions.

ServiceNow offers several methods to manage integrations:

- ServiceNow Store integrations are built by ServiceNow or a partner.
- Integration Hub is the preferred integration solution primarily for outbound integrations from ServiceNow. Integration Hub offers various [pre-built configuration hypers](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=spokes-list.html&version=latest) (called spokes) that give you automation capabilities in a single system of action. Process owners and developers can use IH to build and leverage reusable and expandable integration actions to execute commands against an external system.
- Custom integrations are designed from scratch and enable a specific use case. Customers must own and maintain these long-term.

For detailed planning guidance, please consult our [Integrations Planning Template.](https://nowlearning.servicenow.com/nowcreate?id=nc_asset&asset_id=41f90e45473c5114dbaf1162846d43a1)

Note: ServiceNow does not provide consultation or configuration services for other systems. The customer must ensure an integration Subject Matter Expert (SME) is available.
